# Main game surface

Approved comp: `../mocks/decision/poster-pop-arcade.png`

- World: a friendly local arcade rendered like a late-1970s screen-printed dance poster.
- First viewport: custom wordmark, track controls, equal reference and camera stages, then the readiness/game strip.
- Signature: thick aubergine registration outlines, offset ink shadows, and gesture prompts that feel stamped onto the poster.
- Palette: warm cream paper, coral, turquoise, marigold, lilac, aubergine ink.
- Typography: the locally generated Trance Display face for identity and game moments; native UI sans for dense controls.
- Material: flat paper and screen ink. No glass, glossy cards, or decorative gradients.
- Motion: fast physical press states and existing gameplay animation only; reduced-motion remains supported.
- Preserve: video analysis, hit markers, scoring, one/two player modes, gesture navigation, keyboard/mouse fallbacks, and mobile stacking.
